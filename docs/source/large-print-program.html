Vision for the Future

Thank you for being here tonight.

For more than 90 years, Envision has created opportunities for people who are blind or have low vision to learn, grow, work, and live with greater independence. As we honor the journeys that have shaped our legacy, we also look toward the future: investment in new spaces, emerging technologies, innovative programs, and expanded opportunities that empower individuals to build the futures they choose.

Tonight, we celebrate the impact we have made together and invest in what comes next. Your generosity will help us continue transforming lives, strengthening our community, and creating new possibilities for generations to come.

In the Beren Room

Innovative Assistive Technology Experience

Presented by the Bicknell Envision Vision Rehabilitation Center and Gigi & Carl Allen Envision Research Institute team.

Guests are invited to experience Meta smart glasses through an intimate demonstration led by Envision's clinical and research teams. Discover how emerging assistive technologies are expanding access, independence and possibility for people who are blind or have low vision.

Tactile Art Experience

Presented by the Envision Arts Program.

Experience art through a different perspective. The Beren Room features a curated selection of tactile works presented by the Envision Arts Program, inviting guests to engage with each piece through touch.

Sponsored by HighTouch Technologies, Forvis Mazars, 6 Meridian, Hutton, IMA and Summit Films. Refreshments are served in the Beren Room.

Our Featured Guests

Meet Aubree

Aubree has grown up within the Envision community, participating in programs and experiences designed to build confidence, independence and connection. From childhood through her most recent experience at Level Up, her journey reflects the lasting relationships and opportunities Envision creates across every stage of life.

Meet Adah

Adah's connection to Envision spans years, programs and milestones. After growing up within the Envision community, she returned in a new role as a volunteer intern at the Cathy G. Hudson Envision Child Development Center, an experience that reflects how participation can evolve into leadership, service and a desire to give back.

See Their Stories on Screen Tonight

Meet Hashim

Hashim has been part of the Envision community since childhood, with experiences spanning programs, education, the arts and personal milestones. Now a college graduate, his journey demonstrates how sustained access to opportunity and community can continue to shape a person's path well into adulthood.

Meet Stephen

For nearly 25 years, Stephen has been an integral part of the Envision community, as both a participant in Envision programs and a longtime member of the Envision workforce. His story reflects the enduring power of meaningful employment, connection and opportunity over a lifetime.

Fund-A-Need

Envision Foundation and Mission Services is a nonprofit organization that provides life-enhancing services for all ages and stages of vision loss. 100% of donations go directly to mission services that support people who are blind or have low vision. Tonight, you can make an impact.

Your Impact

$50,000 will fund hundreds of low vision rehabilitation appointments, including orientation and mobility training and occupational therapy, for those who cannot afford care.

$25,000 can support groundbreaking research at the Gigi & Carl Allen Envision Research Institute, improving techniques that benefit millions of people living with low vision.

$15,000 will cover a year's tuition for an infant who is blind or has low vision to attend the Cathy G. Hudson Envision Child Development Center.

$10,000 will fund transportation for teens with vision loss to attend Level Up, where they explore adaptive technology, college, and career options for a successful and independent future.

$5,000 will provide career training and education through the Workforce Innovation Center for a person who is blind or has low vision, offering a life-changing opportunity.

$2,500 will provide supplies and materials for the Envision Arts Program, which transforms the lives of people with vision loss and/or developmental disabilities, improving independence, mental health and well-being.

$1,500 will provide a year's worth of meals for a child who is blind or has low vision at the Cathy G. Hudson Envision Child Development Center.

$500 will provide a white cane and orientation and mobility services for a person who is blind or has low vision in our community.

Welcome

Good evening, and welcome to A Night For Vision.

We are honored to have you with us at the Wichita Art Museum. Tonight, we celebrate the future of Envision, and the remarkable people who are bringing it to life.

Tonight at a glance

  • 5:30 p.m. Check in, cocktails, and experiences
  • 6:30 p.m. Welcome and the Spirit of Philanthropy Award
  • 7:00 p.m. Dinner
  • 7:30 p.m. Fund-A-Need and the chance drawing winner
  • 8:30 p.m. Program ends

Using this guide

  • This guide is yours to explore at your own pace. Wander through the evening in any order you like, and your place will be waiting whenever you return.
  • Every page can be read aloud. Simply select Listen.
  • Nothing will play until you choose, and you may pause at any time from the player at the bottom of your screen.
  • To adjust the size of the text, use the A buttons at the top of the screen. You can also pinch to zoom.
  • For colors and spacing, select Accessibility.

Tonight's featured guests

Throughout the evening, you will meet Aubree, Adah, Hashim, and Stephen, and hear from Envision program participants and employees whose stories reflect what is possible. Together, they are shaping what comes next.
